About me

Shipping a mature product — and bringing AI into how it's built.

I'm a software engineer at Worksoft, working on Certify — a large enterprise test-automation platform used by global companies to validate SAP and other business-critical systems.

My day job spans the full lifecycle of a mature .NET product: shipping customer fixes and hotfixes, debugging production issues across IIS, SQL Server and native dependencies, and helping move a 120+ project codebase from .NET Framework 4.8 to .NET 8 and .NET 10.

Alongside that, I build with AI every day — MCP servers in C# that expose product APIs to LLM agents, agentic workflows with Claude Code, and AI-assisted test creation.
